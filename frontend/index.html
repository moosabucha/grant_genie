Algorithms
Three methods. Best one wins.
All three algorithms run simultaneously. The one with the highest F1-Score on the labeled dataset is selected automatically.
Algorithm A

TF-IDF

Converts documents into numerical vectors and calculates cosine similarity. Excellent for capturing overall topic alignment between your profile and a grant's focus area.

F1: 0.70
Algorithm B

RapidFuzz

Fuzzy keyword matching that handles abbreviations and terminology variations. Catches matches that TF-IDF misses when wording differs between your profile and the grant text.

F1: 0.69
Best performer
Algorithm C

Hybrid

A weighted combination of TF-IDF and RapidFuzz. Balances topic-level and keyword-level matching to produce the most accurate relevance scores across all grant types.

F1: 0.84
